## Overview

Notzeee is a lightweight notes application designed for simplicity, speed, and a premium writing experience.

## What Users Can Do

- **Create Notes**: Write and manage personal notes.
- **Clean UI**: Distraction-free writing interface.
- **Edit Easily**: Fast updates without page reloads.
- **Responsive Design**: Works across screen sizes.

## Why I Built This

- Most notes apps feel cluttered.
- I wanted a minimal, writing-first experience.
- To practice frontend state management and UX clarity.

- React
- JavaScript

## After Launch & Impact

- Improved UX design sense.
- Learned to balance minimalism with usability.
